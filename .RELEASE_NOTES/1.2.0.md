# 1.2.0 - 2026-08-11

## Highlights
- SEO overhaul: real product detail pages, structured data, sitemap/robots, and full CTA analytics coverage. Indexable URLs go from 2 to 14 (P-009).

## Added
- Product detail pages for Cleopatra Noir, Georgia Flora, and Croesus Gold — price, specs, scent notes, persona story, and per-product FAQ, served from one dynamic route `app/[lang]/[slug]` (P-009).
- Product catalog in `lib/products.ts` — price, size, concentration, longevity, SKU, and a `published` flag that drives the homepage grid and sitemap.
- Trust pages: `/tentang-kami`, `/pengiriman-pengembalian`, `/kebijakan-privasi`, sharing one `ContentPage` renderer (P-009).
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` via `app/sitemap.ts` and `app/robots.ts`, with hreflang alternates per entry.
- JSON-LD structured data — Organization, WebSite, Product, BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage (`lib/schema.ts`, `components/JsonLd.tsx`).
- Open Graph and Twitter card metadata, plus a locale-aware generated OG image (`app/[lang]/opengraph-image.tsx`).
- Canonical URLs and hreflang (`id`, `en`, `x-default`) on every page, driven by `lib/site.ts`.
- Ingredients section on the homepage naming the 18 materials used across the collection.
- `qrUrl()` helper for packaging QR codes — locale-less short slugs with UTM tags.
- Shared `SiteFooter` and `ProductCard` components.

## Changed
- Locale routing no longer sniffs `Accept-Language`. Every unprefixed path now 308-redirects to `/id`, so Googlebot (which crawls from US IPs) sees the Indonesian site as primary rather than the English one (P-009).
- Homepage product cards link to their product pages instead of the `#shop` anchor.
- Analytics coverage extended to the hero CTA, product cards, nav shop button, and language switcher — none of which fired events before. Product interactions now emit GA4's standard `select_item` with `item_id`.
- Outbound Shopee and TikTok links carry UTM parameters for marketplace-side attribution. WhatsApp links deliberately excluded, since extra query keys break the prefilled message.
- Language switcher preserves the current path instead of dropping to the locale root.
- Hero poster preloaded with `fetchPriority="high"` to improve mobile LCP.
- Pricing set to Rp 99.000 with Rp 140.000 as the compare-at price; volume set to 50 ml and concentration to Eau de Parfum (EDP).
- Longevity stated as 6-8 hours on skin and up to 12 hours on fabric, replacing the flat "12 jam" claim.
- Em dashes removed from all user-facing copy.
- Linktree page (`/links`) localized and set to `noindex, follow`.

## Fixed
- Product routes `/cleopatra-noir`, `/georgia-flora`, and `/croesus-gold` were 6-line redirect stubs that bounced to the homepage and were linked from nowhere. They now serve real pages.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` were caught by the proxy matcher and would have been redirected into a locale path; both are now excluded.
- Untranslated English strings leaking into the Indonesian experience — linktree copy, image alt text, and several aria-labels.
- GA4 `debug_mode` support added, so DebugView works on a real device via `?ga_debug=1` (always on in development).

## Notes
- `rose-antoinette` is a real SKU still in R&D. Its route now returns 404 and the slug is reserved in `lib/products.ts` with `published: false`; flipping that flag adds the page, the sitemap entry, and the homepage card automatically.
- Product notes, SKU codes, and the persona stories on each product page are drafts and should be verified against the actual product before wide promotion.
- Trust pages omit legal entity name, business address, and email — these were not invented and need to be filled in.
- No BPOM claims anywhere; the brand is not yet registered.
- Only Cleopatra Noir has more than one product image.
- `vercel.json` still has `git.deploymentEnabled: false`, so this release requires a manual deploy.
- Post-deploy setup (Search Console, GA4 custom dimensions, data retention) is tracked in `.plans/P-009_non_coding_checklist.md`.
