# 1.3.0 - 2026-08-11

## Highlights
- Optimizes the site for Google AI Overviews and LLM answer engines: a new Indonesian guide section, rewritten FAQ answers that survive being quoted out of context, and article-level structured data. Indexable URLs go from 14 to 21 (P-010).

## Added
- `/id/panduan` guide hub plus six Indonesian guides: what Eau de Parfum means, how to make perfume last, why perfume fades, the scent pyramid explained, perfume for hot weather, and choosing perfume by occasion (`lib/guides.ts`, `app/[lang]/panduan`).
- Each guide opens with a self-contained answer block sized for passage extraction, followed by question-shaped section headings, its own FAQ rendered as open prose, and cross-links to related guides and the collection.
- `Article`, `AboutPage`, and `ItemList` JSON-LD builders (`lib/schema.ts`). Guides emit `Article` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage`; trust pages now emit `AboutPage`/`WebPage` instead of breadcrumbs alone.
- Visible "Diperbarui" dates with `<time>` elements and a brand byline on guides and trust pages, plus `datePublished`/`dateModified` in schema. The site previously had no date or authorship signal anywhere.
- `scripts/check-passages.mjs` — asserts every FAQ answer names its subject in the first sentence, avoids a bare affirmative opener, and lands in the 30-60 word band that extracts cleanly. Fails per-answer.
- `scripts/check-metadata.mjs` — audits title and description lengths against truncation limits and flags duplicates across pages.
- `canonicalDefaultLocaleOnly()` and a `CONTENT_UPDATED` constant (`lib/site.ts`).
- Footer link to the guide hub, shown on the Indonesian locale only.

## Changed
- All 20 FAQ answers rewritten in both locales to stand alone when quoted. Answers previously opened with a bare "Ya." or a subjectless fragment like "Di kulit biasanya 6-8 jam", which loses all meaning once an answer engine renders it away from its question. Five questions were also rephrased to name their subject.
- FAQ questions are now `<h3>` inside `<summary>` on the homepage and product pages, giving them real heading semantics without changing the accordion behavior.
- Product page scent pyramid changed from `<ul>` with styled paragraph labels to a `<dl>`, so each tier label stays bound to its notes.
- Product page spec and buy blocks now carry `<h2>` headings, using the `pdp.specsTitle` and `pdp.buyTitle` dictionary keys that already existed but were rendered nowhere.
- Homepage values section given a real heading instead of only an `aria-label`.
- Sitemap grew to 21 URLs and now includes the guide hub and guides.

## Fixed
- `sitemap.ts` emitted `lastModified: new Date()`, so all URLs claimed to change on every deploy including CSS-only ones. Dates now come from content (`guide.updated`, `CONTENT_UPDATED`). A `lastmod` that always reads "now" is a signal Google learns to discount.
- `WebSite` JSON-LD used one `@id` (`/#website`) across both locales while emitting different `url` and `inLanguage` values, so `/id` and `/en` contradicted each other in the entity graph. The id is now locale-scoped.
- Six guide meta titles exceeded the truncation limit once the `%s | HASERA` title template appended nine characters. Shortened; all 16 audited pages now sit within title and description limits with no duplicates.

## Notes
- Guides are Indonesian only by design. `/en/panduan` and `/en/panduan/*` return 404, and guide pages deliberately emit no `hreflang`, since pointing an alternate at a 404 is worse than omitting it. Switching `canonicalDefaultLocaleOnly` back to `alternatesFor` is what would enable English later.
- There is no such thing as AI Overviews markup. This release is ordinary SEO applied to passage clarity, entity legibility, and informational coverage — the three things that actually determine whether an answer engine can quote you.
- Being cited in AI Overviews often means fewer clicks, not more. Treat this as brand and entity work rather than a traffic channel.
- Google Search Console does not break out AI Overviews impressions or clicks; they are folded into normal Web Search performance. Measurement is manual spot-checks plus impressions-up/CTR-down as a proxy.
- The ~3,000 words of Indonesian guide copy are a first draft written to the template. Claims are conservative and use industry-standard concentration ranges, but the perfume advice should be reviewed by someone who formulates.
- The guide hub is reachable from the footer and cross-links only; it is not yet in the main nav.
- `package.json` still reads `1.2.0` and needs bumping before tagging.
- `vercel.json` still has `git.deploymentEnabled: false`, so this release requires a manual deploy.
